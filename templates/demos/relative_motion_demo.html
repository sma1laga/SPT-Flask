Why a pass repeats every 4 h
In 4 hours, the satellite must gain an integer number of full turns on the station:

Interpretation
m = 1: the satellite gains 360° on the station in 4 hours.
m = 2: it gains 720° in 4 hours, so it still lines up after 4 hours.
The repeat interval is set by the relative motion, not directly by the orbital period.
